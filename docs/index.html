It presses ⌘V and Enter for you. That's all it does.

macOS asks once for Accessibility, the permission that lets an app press keys. The free version has no account, no network calls and no analytics. Your keys live on your Mac, and the code is on GitHub.

Questions.

Which apps does it work with?

Anything with a text box: Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Gemini, Codex, Claude Code, Terminal, your browser.

Is it really free?

Yes. Keys, shortcuts and your own words are free and open source. Pro will add voice and queues.

Windows or Linux?

Mac only for now, on Apple Silicon with macOS 14 or newer.

Why does the download say Agent Deck?

That was its first name. The next release ships as Agent Bar.
