It presses ⌘V and Enter for you. No account, no server.

macOS asks once for Accessibility, the permission that lets an app press keys, and for the microphone when you talk. There's no account, no analytics and no Agent Bar server. Voice goes to Apple's dictation or, with your own key, straight to OpenAI. Your keys and history live on your Mac, and the code is on GitHub.

Questions.

How do I install it?

Open AgentBar.dmg and drag Agent Bar into Applications, then open it. It isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time macOS says it can't check it: press Done, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and press «Open Anyway». A two-minute tutorial then sets everything up.

Which apps does it work with?

Anything with a text box: Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Gemini, Codex, Claude Code, Terminal, your browser.

Is it really free?

Yes. Keys, voice with fn, prompts and alerts are free and open source. OpenAI voice uses your own key. Pro will add voice without a key, autopilot and iPhone.

Do I need an OpenAI key to talk?

No. Without one, fn uses the Mac's dictation. With a key, the text comes out cleaner and long requests turn into a prompt.

How do alerts know my agent is done?

Claude Code tells it. One click adds two hooks to your Claude Code settings, with a backup first. The hook waits a few minutes for your reply and hands it to that conversation. Nothing guesses or reads your screen.

Windows or Linux?

Mac only for now, on Apple Silicon with macOS 14 or newer.

Why does the download say Agent Deck?

That was its first name. The app is becoming Agent Bar; everything works the same.
